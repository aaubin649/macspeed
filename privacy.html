MacSpeed

Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

MacSpeed doesn't collect, send or sell any of your data. Everything it measures stays on your Mac.

What MacSpeed reads

To find what's slowing your Mac, MacSpeed reads the list of running processes, memory, CPU, heat, battery and disk usage, your startup items, and the size of cache folders. Space Map and Duplicates read the folders you choose to scan. This information is used only to show you results on your Mac.

What it stores

Scan history, benchmark results and the startup items you disabled are saved in your own Library folder (~/Library/Application Support/MacSpeed), so you can compare results and restore items. Deleting the app and that folder removes everything.

Network

Once a day, MacSpeed checks for a new version by downloading one small file from GitHub. The request sends nothing about you or your Mac: no ID, no scan results. GitHub counts how many times that file is downloaded, which is how we know roughly how many people use MacSpeed. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website (see GitHub's privacy statement). You can turn the check off in Settings.

The paid version will contact our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, only to check a license key you enter. That request includes the key and a generic device name, nothing about your Mac's contents.

Analytics

No tracking, no crash reporting, no ads. The only usage number is the update-check count above.

Website

This website has no cookies or analytics. The host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
