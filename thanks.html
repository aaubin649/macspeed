Activate it

  1. Open MacSpeed. If you don't have it yet, download it here.
  2. In the menu bar, choose MacSpeed › Settings (or press ⌘,).
  3. Paste your key under MacSpeed Pro and click Activate.

Your key works on 2 Macs. To move it to a new Mac, click Deactivate on This Mac in Settings first.

Something wrong?

Can't find the key or it won't activate? Open an issue or reply to the receipt email. Refunds go through Polar.
